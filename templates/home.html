About the system

From ESP32 sensors to the buyer's hands.

Soil-moisture probes, a DHT22 weather unit and a rain sensor stream live data from an ESP32 node. The auto-irrigation engine drives a relay and pump across three zones, every harvest gets a printable QR tag, and buyers trace produce from field to shelf.
